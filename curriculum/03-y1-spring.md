# Year 1, Spring

**Level:** UNT Level 2.1  
**Focus:** Second line, brushes at every tempo, big band Latin, and 6/8.  
**Source:** UNT 2022-23 drum set syllabus

## Courses this mirrors
- MUAC 15xx Lessons
- MULB 18xx Large Ensemble
- MUTH 1500 Theory II
- MUTH 1510 Aural Skills II
- MUAG 1012 Keyboard Skills II
- MUJS 1132 Jazz Performance Fundamentals II

## Barrier materials
- Soph, Big Band Primer p.29-34
- Wilcoxon, Modern Rudimental Swing Solos p.14
- Helbing, Studies in Syncopation p.19-20
- Helbing, Big Band Sight Reading (etudes 7, 11, 13, 22, 24)
- Davis, Vocabulary #2 (UNT handout), 1-8
- All tunes and play-alongs

## Goals
- New Orleans second-line grooves
- Brushes: medium swing, ballad, up-tempo
- Samba, songo, and mambo in a big band setting
- Solo with Vocabulary #2
