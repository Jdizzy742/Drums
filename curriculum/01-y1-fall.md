# Year 1, Fall

**UNT equivalent:** Level 1  
**Focus:** The ride cymbal is the center of the band.

## UNT courses this mirrors
- Applied Drum Set (private lessons)
- MUTH 1400/1500 Theory I + Aural Skills I
- MUJS 1131 Jazz Performance Fundamentals
- Keyboard Skills I
- Large ensemble (lab band)

## Goals
- Steady swing time from 80 to 180 bpm
- Basic comping on snare and bass drum
- Medium-swing brushes
- Hear and keep form on blues and AABA tunes

## Drum set (applied lessons)

### Unit 1: Ride cymbal (weeks 1-3)
- [ ] Ride pattern alone with click on 2 and 4, 80 to 180 bpm: even, swung, relaxed
- [ ] Add hi-hat 2 and 4 plus feathered bass drum
- [ ] Listen and copy: play along with 'So What' (Jimmy Cobb) and 'Moanin'' (Blakey)

### Unit 2: Comping (weeks 4-6)
- [ ] Syncopation (Reed) syncopation lessons read on snare under ride and hi-hat, 100-140 bpm
- [ ] Same exercises read on bass drum under ride and hi-hat
- [ ] John Riley, The Art of Bop Drumming: first comping exercises

### Unit 3: Brushes and Latin (weeks 7-9)
- [ ] Medium-swing brush pattern (sweep plus 2 and 4 pat), 100-140 bpm
- [ ] Bossa nova: cross-stick clave, bass drum 1 and 3 pattern, 120-160 bpm
- [ ] Basic samba on the kit (Ed Soph Big Band Primer or similar), 80-100 bpm half-note feel

### Unit 4: Fills and trading (weeks 10-12)
- [ ] Two-bar fills in swing that set up beat 1
- [ ] Trade 4s over blues and AABA, keep your place in the form
- [ ] Wilcoxon, The All-American Drummer: solos 1-5 on pad
- [ ] Stick Control p.5-7 exercises 1-24 at 100 bpm

## Musicianship (theory, ear, keyboard)
- [ ] Theory I: intervals, major and minor scales, triads
- [ ] Aural I: sing major scale and intervals up to a 5th
- [ ] Keyboard I: C, F, G major scales hands separately
- [ ] Sing the melody of each tune while playing time
- [ ] Transcribe: write out 8 bars of Jimmy Cobb's ride and comping on 'So What'

## Listening
- Miles Davis, Milestones (Philly Joe Jones)
- Sonny Rollins, Saxophone Colossus (Max Roach)
- Oscar Peterson, Night Train (Ed Thigpen, brushes)

## Tunes to learn
- Blue Monk
- Now's the Time
- Autumn Leaves
- Satin Doll

## Jury (weeks 13-16)
Record yourself. Pass every item before moving on.

- [ ] Swing time at 100, 140, and 180 bpm, 2 choruses each
- [ ] Comp from a Syncopation page at 120 bpm
- [ ] Brushes, medium swing, 2 choruses
- [ ] Bossa nova and samba grooves, 16 bars each
- [ ] Trade 4s over a full tune with a play-along
- [ ] Sight-read a short snare etude
