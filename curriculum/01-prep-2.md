# Prep 2: Deficient Level

**Level:** UNT Deficient  
**Focus:** Reading, shuffle, and comping in samba and songo feels.  
**Source:** UNT 2022-23 drum set syllabus

## Courses this mirrors
- UNT applied lessons, Deficient barrier (some students start here, some start at Level 1)

## Barrier materials
- Soph, Musical Time (ex. 1-70), all with samba and songo bass drum
- Reed, Syncopation (p.10 and p.38)
- Morgan, The Jazz Drummer's Reading Workbook (p.7-22, 29-32)

**Tempos:** Essential Techniques: quarter = 40-120. Basic Duplet: quarter = 50-90.

## Goals
- Shuffle and triplet variations from Reed p.10
- Reed p.38 exercise 1 several ways, up to 120 bpm
- Read big band style rhythms from Morgan
- Transcribe a drummer's comping

## Weekly lessons

### Week 1
- [ ] Soph, Musical Time ex. 1-10
- [ ] Reed p.10: shuffle bass drum, shuffle left hand, 2nd and 3rd triplets in left hand
- [ ] Morgan p.7-8
- [ ] Play-along: Freddie Freeloader

### Week 2
- [ ] Soph ex. 11-20
- [ ] Reed p.10: shuffle bass drum, shuffle left hand, triplets in left hand
- [ ] Morgan p.9-10
- [ ] Play-along: Freddie Freeloader

### Week 3
- [ ] Soph ex. 21-30
- [ ] Reed p.10 variations continued
- [ ] Morgan p.11-12
- [ ] Play-along: In the Mood

### Week 4
- [ ] Soph ex. 21-30
- [ ] Reed p.38 ex. 1 three ways (snare, bass drum, snare/bass unison), 50-100 bpm
- [ ] Morgan p.13-14
- [ ] Play-along: In the Mood

### Week 5
- [ ] Soph ex. 31-40 (review ex. 15-17)
- [ ] Reed p.38 ex. 1 three ways, 50-100 bpm
- [ ] Morgan p.15-16
- [ ] Play-along: Jumpin' at the Woodside

### Week 6
- [ ] Soph ex. 31-40
- [ ] Reed p.38 alternating snare/bass and bass/snare, 50-85 bpm
- [ ] Morgan p.17-19
- [ ] Play-along: Jumpin' at the Woodside

### Week 7
- [ ] Soph ex. 41-50
- [ ] Reed p.38 alternating, 85-120 bpm, with samba bass drum
- [ ] Morgan p.21-22
- [ ] Play-along: Walkin'

### Week 8
- [ ] Soph ex. 41-50
- [ ] Reed p.38 ex. 1 with samba bass drum (hands unison, ride pattern, reverse ride pattern)
- [ ] Morgan p.29-30
- [ ] Play-along: Walkin'

### Week 9
- [ ] Soph ex. 51-60
- [ ] Reed p.38 ex. 1 with samba bass drum
- [ ] Morgan p.31-32
- [ ] Play-along: Autumn Leaves

### Week 10
- [ ] Soph ex. 51-60
- [ ] Reed p.38 ex. 1 with samba and songo bass drum
- [ ] Play-along: Autumn Leaves

### Week 11
- [ ] Soph ex. 61-70
- [ ] Reed p.38 ex. 1 with songo bass drum, orchestrated around the drums
- [ ] Play-along: This I Dig of You

### Week 12
- [ ] Soph ex. 61-70, review all
- [ ] Play-along: This I Dig of You

### Week 13
- [ ] Review everything for the jury

## Comping transcription
- [ ] Week 3: Choose your jury transcription
- [ ] Week 5: First draft written out
- [ ] Week 8: Revised draft
- [ ] Week 10: Playable at a slow tempo
- [ ] Week 12: Memorized and playable with the recording
- [ ] Week 13: Record it and save the video (UNT: upload)

## Musicianship (classroom courses)
- [ ] Key signatures and all 12 major scales (written)
- [ ] Learn the piano keyboard layout; play C, F, G major scales
- [ ] Sing the melody of every play-along tune

## Listening
- Miles Davis, Kind of Blue (Freddie Freeloader)
- Count Basie, Jumpin' at the Woodside
- Miles Davis, Walkin' (Kenny Clarke)
- Cannonball Adderley, Somethin' Else (Autumn Leaves, Art Blakey)
- Hank Mobley, Soul Station (This I Dig of You, Art Blakey)

## Tunes

- In the Mood

- Autumn Leaves

Work every tune through the [tune routine](../README.md#tune-routine).

## Jury
Record yourself. Pass every item before moving on.

- [ ] Any Soph Musical Time exercise 1-70 on request, with samba and songo bass drum
- [ ] Reed p.10 shuffle and triplet variations
- [ ] Reed p.38 ex. 1, all variations, up to 120 bpm
- [ ] Sight-read from Morgan
- [ ] Comping transcription played with the recording
- [ ] Any play-along from the list
