# Year 2, Spring

**UNT equivalent:** Level 3.1  
**Focus:** The sophomore checkpoint. Odd meters and jazz history.

## UNT courses this mirrors
- Applied Drum Set
- MUJS 2500/2510 Jazz Theory IV + Aural Skills IV
- MUJS Jazz Improvisation II
- MUJS 3070 History of Jazz
- MUJS 1014 Keyboard Skills IV
- Jazz ensemble or combo

## Goals
- Comfortable in 5/4 and 7/4
- Broken-time and interactive comping
- Up-tempo time at 260 bpm
- Know the drummers who shaped each jazz era

## Drum set (applied lessons)

### Unit 1: Odd meters (weeks 1-3)
- [ ] 5/4 swing: play 'Take Five' with the record
- [ ] 7/4 as 4+3 and 3+4, swing and straight
- [ ] Count and sing the subdivisions while playing

### Unit 2: Beyond bop (weeks 4-6)
- [ ] John Riley, Beyond Bop Drumming: broken-time studies
- [ ] Comp in response to a soloist (play-along with a horn solo)
- [ ] Up-tempo: 220 to 260 bpm, lighter touch

### Unit 3: Jazz history (drummers) (weeks 7-9)
- [ ] New Orleans and swing: Baby Dodds, Chick Webb, Jo Jones
- [ ] Bebop: Kenny Clarke, Max Roach, Roy Haynes
- [ ] Hard bop and post-bop: Art Blakey, Philly Joe Jones, Elvin Jones, Tony Williams
- [ ] Play 8 bars in each drummer's style

### Unit 4: Funk and hands (weeks 10-12)
- [ ] Steve Gadd and Bernard Purdie-style grooves
- [ ] Wilcoxon, Modern Rudimental Swing Solos: 2 solos
- [ ] Transcribe Art Blakey's solo on 'Moanin''

## Musicianship (theory, ear, keyboard)
- [ ] Jazz Theory IV: reharmonization basics, modal harmony
- [ ] Aural IV: hear form changes and key changes in a tune
- [ ] Improv II: sing a 2-chorus solo over blues
- [ ] History: build a 20-track timeline playlist, one drummer per track

## Listening
- Dave Brubeck, Time Out (Joe Morello)
- Miles Davis, Four and More (Tony Williams)
- John Coltrane, Coltrane's Sound (Elvin Jones)

## Tunes to learn
- Take Five
- Speak No Evil
- Cherokee
- Chameleon

## Jury (weeks 13-16)
Record yourself. Pass every item before moving on.

- [ ] Sophomore checkpoint: 15-min program with 3 contrasting tunes
- [ ] Swing time at 260 bpm
- [ ] 5/4 and 7/4 grooves with fills
- [ ] Play the Blakey transcription with the record
- [ ] Explain and demonstrate 3 drummers' styles
