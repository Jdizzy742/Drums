# Year 4, Fall

**Level:** UNT Level 4.2  
**Focus:** Afro-Cuban styles, built on the clave.  
**Source:** UNT 2022-23 drum set syllabus

## Courses this mirrors
- MUAC 35xx Lessons
- MULB 18xx Large Ensemble
- MUCM 3550 Jazz Chamber Music
- MUET 3030 Music Cultures of the World
- MUJS 3470 Jazz Lecture Series

## Barrier materials
- Hernandez, Conversations in Clave (assigned pages)
- Lopez-Nusa, Ritmos de Cuba (assigned pages)
- Aponte, Styles (UNT handouts)
- Sher Music, The Latin Jazz Real Book

## Goals
- Clave as the organizing concept
- 6/8, rumba, danzon, son, mambo, cha-cha-cha
- Mozambique, salsa, songo, timba on the kit

## Weekly lessons

### Week 1: Clave
- [ ] The clave concept
- [ ] Hernandez p.13-18, 23-26

### Week 2: Afro-Cuban 6/8
- [ ] Bembe and Abakua
- [ ] Hernandez p.61-67 (6/8 coordination); Lopez-Nusa p.77, 81
- [ ] Tune: Afro Blue (LRB p.7) or Eastern Joy Dance (LRB p.187)

### Week 3: Rumba
- [ ] Yambu, guaguanco, columbia
- [ ] Hernandez p.117-119 (rumba coordination)
- [ ] Tune: Dejala Que Baile Sola (LRB p.153) or Dime Tu Que Lo Sabes (LRB p.173)

### Week 4: Danzon and baqueteo
- [ ] Hernandez p.117-119 (baqueteo); Lopez-Nusa p.92-93
- [ ] Tune: Almendra (LRB p.13) or Danzon for My Father (LRB p.147)

### Week 5: Son: cascara
- [ ] Hernandez p.81-85; Lopez-Nusa p.98-99
- [ ] Tune: Dile a Catalina (LRB p.169) or Camina y Prende el Fogon (LRB p.107)

### Week 6: Son: campana
- [ ] Hernandez p.88-89 (bongo bell systems); Lopez-Nusa p.98-99

### Week 7: Mambo
- [ ] Mambo bell flow; Hernandez p.86-90; Lopez-Nusa p.96-97
- [ ] Tune: Mambo #5 (LRB p.315), Sabor (LRB p.443), or Mambo Inn (LRB p.321)

### Week 8: Cha-cha-cha
- [ ] Cha bell flow; Hernandez p.117-119; Lopez-Nusa p.94-95
- [ ] Tune: La Enganadora (LRB p.265) or Calzada del Cerro (LRB p.115)

### Week 9: Mozambique
- [ ] Cuban and New York approaches; Hernandez p.117-119; Lopez-Nusa p.114-115
- [ ] Tune: Bamboleate (Palmieri) or El Mozambique (Pello el Afrokan)

### Week 10: Salsa format
- [ ] Salsa drum set applications; review Hernandez p.81-85, 88-89
- [ ] Tune: Sin Tu Carino (LRB p.467) or Indestructible (LRB p.241)

### Week 11: Songo
- [ ] Hernandez p.111, 117-119; Lopez-Nusa p.104-107
- [ ] Tune: Lo Que Va a Pasar (LRB p.293) or Sandunguera (LRB p.449)

### Week 12: Timba
- [ ] Timba drum set approach; Hernandez p.77-79
- [ ] Tune: Que Sorpresa (LRB p.427) or Un Tipo Como Yo (LRB p.515)

### Week 13
- [ ] Review for jury
