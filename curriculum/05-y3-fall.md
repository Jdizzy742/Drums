# Year 3, Fall

**UNT equivalent:** Level 3.2  
**Focus:** Polyrhythm, recording yourself, and leading a combo.

## UNT courses this mirrors
- Applied Drum Set
- MUJS 3360 Advanced Jazz Improvisation I
- MUJS Jazz Arranging I
- Jazz Chamber Music (combo)
- MUAE 3100 DAW / recording
- MUJS 3470 Jazz Lecture Series

## Goals
- Elvin-style triplet phrasing and 3-over-4
- Record and mix a trio play-along
- Lead intros, endings, and count-offs
- Gospel chops vocabulary

## Drum set (applied lessons)

### Unit 1: Triplet concept (weeks 1-3)
- [ ] Triplets split between hands and feet in swing
- [ ] 3-over-4 polyrhythm: dotted quarter on ride over swing
- [ ] Transcribe one chorus of Elvin Jones comping on 'Crescent'

### Unit 2: Recording (weeks 4-6)
- [ ] Set up a DAW (GarageBand, Reaper, or Logic)
- [ ] Mic the kit: 2-4 mics (overheads plus kick)
- [ ] Record and mix yourself with a trio play-along

### Unit 3: Combo leadership (weeks 7-9)
- [ ] Count-offs at any tempo, steady from the first beat
- [ ] Common intros and endings (tag, vamp, Basie ending)
- [ ] Play with real musicians: join a jam session or community band

### Unit 4: Gospel chops (weeks 10-12)
- [ ] Fast singles and doubles around the kit (sextuplets)
- [ ] Hand-foot combinations (RLK, RLRLKK)
- [ ] Gospel shed-style fills inside a groove, musically placed

## Musicianship (theory, ear, keyboard)
- [ ] Advanced Improv I: sing and play rhythm of a famous horn solo
- [ ] Arranging I: write a lead sheet arrangement of a blues with intro and ending
- [ ] Write a drum chart for your arrangement
- [ ] Lecture series: watch 3 drummer clinics and take notes

## Listening
- John Coltrane, Crescent (Elvin Jones)
- Herbie Hancock, Maiden Voyage (Tony Williams)
- Kirk Franklin and gospel drum shed videos (gospel chops vocabulary)

## Tunes to learn
- Crescent
- Maiden Voyage
- Inner Urge
- Joy Spring

## Jury (weeks 13-16)
Record yourself. Pass every item before moving on.

- [ ] Play the Elvin transcription with the record
- [ ] Submit a recorded and mixed trio track
- [ ] Lead a tune with your own intro and ending
- [ ] Demonstrate 3-over-4 in time
- [ ] A musical 16-bar gospel chops feature
