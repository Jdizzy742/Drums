# Prep 1: Foundations

**Level:** Pre-UNT (beginner)  
**Focus:** Hands, time, and reading.  
**Source:** Self-study prep (not a UNT level)

## Courses this mirrors
- Not a UNT level. Gets a beginner ready to start UNT's Deficient level.

## Barrier materials
- Steady time with a click
- Basic rudiments
- Quarter and eighth-note reading

## Goals
- Play steady time with a click without drifting
- Read quarter, eighth, and simple syncopated rhythms
- Play a basic jazz ride pattern with hi-hat on 2 and 4
- Know the form of a 12-bar blues

## Weekly lessons

### Week 1-3: Grip and strokes
- [ ] Matched grip, fulcrum, and rebound on a practice pad (check in a mirror)
- [ ] Four basic strokes: full, down, tap, up. Quarter = 60-80
- [ ] Single strokes (RLRL) eighth notes, 60 to 100 bpm
- [ ] Double strokes (RRLL) eighth notes, 60 to 90 bpm
- [ ] Kit setup: throne height, pedal and cymbal positions, basic tuning

### Week 4-6: Time and basic grooves
- [ ] Eighth-note rock groove, 70 to 110 bpm, 2 min unbroken with click
- [ ] 8 bass drum variations under that groove
- [ ] One-bar fills on snare and toms that land on beat 1 cleanly
- [ ] Click on 2 and 4 only: feel it as the backbeat for 1 minute

### Week 7-9: Reading
- [ ] Count out loud: quarters, eighths, rests, ties (1 e & a)
- [ ] Reed, Syncopation: first quarter- and eighth-note reading lessons on snare, 60-80 bpm
- [ ] Stone, Stick Control p.5 exercises 1-12 at 80 bpm
- [ ] Rudiments: paradiddle, flam, drag, five-stroke roll, slow and even

### Week 10-12: First jazz time
- [ ] Jazz ride pattern with hi-hat on 2 and 4, 100-140 bpm
- [ ] Feather the bass drum on all 4 beats (barely audible)
- [ ] Play time through a 12-bar blues play-along, counting bars out loud
- [ ] Preview Reed p.10: shuffle on bass drum, very slow

## Musicianship (classroom courses)
- [ ] Note names on treble and bass clef (musictheory.net)
- [ ] Major scale construction (W-W-H-W-W-W-H)
- [ ] Song forms: 12-bar blues and 32-bar AABA

## Listening
- Miles Davis, Kind of Blue (Jimmy Cobb)
- Art Blakey and the Jazz Messengers, Moanin' (Art Blakey)
- Count Basie, The Atomic Mr. Basie (Sonny Payne)

## Tunes
- C Jam Blues
- Sonnymoon for Two

Work every tune through the [tune routine](../README.md#tune-routine).

## Jury
Record yourself. Pass every item before moving on.

- [ ] Rock groove, 100 bpm, 2 min with click, fill every 8 bars
- [ ] Jazz time over 3 choruses of blues at 120 bpm, no lost form
- [ ] Singles, doubles, paradiddles on pad at 90 bpm eighth notes
- [ ] Read one page of Reed at 70 bpm without stopping
