# Year 3, Spring

**Level:** UNT Level 4.1  
**Focus:** Odd and mixed meter, open-handed playing, funk, soul, and Motown.  
**Source:** UNT 2022-23 drum set syllabus

## Courses this mirrors
- MUAC 35xx Lessons
- MULB 18xx Large Ensemble
- MUJS 3370 Advanced Jazz Improvisation II
- MUJS 3620 Jazz Arranging
- MUCM 3550 Jazz Chamber Music
- MUMH 3520 Issues in Western Music History

## Barrier materials
- Helbing, Studies in Syncopation p.19, 20, 83, 85, 89, 94, 97-100
- Garibaldi, Future Sounds p.24, 29, 32, 33 (open-handed)
- Davis, Triplets Between the Limbs p.1 (open-handed)
- Play-alongs: Mother Popcorn, Get Together, Kate Song, Cissy Strut, Cold Sweat, Oakland Stroke, Sofa Song, Dream Thief, Dreaming
- All tunes

## Goals
- Play grooves in 5/4, 7/4, 9/8, 11/8, 12/8, 13/8
- Open-handed versions of earlier material
- Put a standard into 3, 5, or 7
- Funk, soul, Motown, and Afrobeat feels

## Weekly lessons

### Week 1
- [ ] Plan a professional audition (UNT: discuss audition)
- [ ] Helbing p.83 on snare, then around the kit
- [ ] Garibaldi p.24 open-handed
- [ ] Helbing p.19 swing variations, open-handed
- [ ] Play-alongs: Take Five (5/4), Mother Popcorn (funk), Get Together (Afrobeat)

### Week 2
- [ ] Helbing p.85 on snare, around the kit
- [ ] Garibaldi p.29 open-handed
- [ ] Helbing p.20 swing variations, open-handed
- [ ] Play-alongs: Cherokee (Ahmad Jamal, up-tempo brushes), Kate Song (straight 5/4), Cissy Strut, Cold Sweat

### Week 3
- [ ] Helbing p.89 on snare, around the kit
- [ ] Garibaldi p.32 open-handed
- [ ] Triplets Between the Limbs p.1, open-handed
- [ ] Play-alongs: Young and Playful (7/4), Mean Streets (up-tempo brushes), Soul Vaccination, Oakland Stroke

### Week 4
- [ ] Helbing p.94 on snare, around the kit
- [ ] Garibaldi p.33 open-handed
- [ ] Put a standard into 3, 5, or 7 (Night and Day, Cherokee, How High the Moon, Autumn Leaves, It Might As Well Be Spring)
- [ ] Play-alongs: 50 Ways to Leave Your Lover (orchestrate different ways), Home at Last (Purdie shuffle)

### Week 5
- [ ] Standard in 3, 5, or 7, continued
- [ ] Play-alongs: Sofa Song (9/8), Blue Matter (slow groove), Isn't She Lovely (12/8 funk)

### Week 6
- [ ] Standard in 3, 5, or 7, playable
- [ ] Play-alongs: Punjab, The Dream Thief (11/8), Please Mr. Postman, I Can't Help Myself, Get Ready (Motown)

### Week 7
- [ ] Helbing p.97: orchestrate, create a groove from the mixed-meter grid
- [ ] Play-alongs: A Shade of Jade, The Dream Thief, I Can't Stand the Rain, Superstition, Let's Stay Together

### Week 8
- [ ] Helbing p.98: mixed-meter grid groove
- [ ] Play-alongs: Dreaming (13/8), Rock with You, Afro-Centric

### Week 9
- [ ] Helbing p.99: mixed-meter grid groove
- [ ] Play-alongs: Straight Street, Dreaming, Aja (orchestrating funk)

### Week 10
- [ ] Helbing p.100: mixed-meter grid groove
- [ ] Play-alongs: Humpty Dumpty; As This Moment Slips Away (The Bad Plus, transcribe the mixed-meter section)

### Week 11-13
- [ ] Review for jury and barrier exam
