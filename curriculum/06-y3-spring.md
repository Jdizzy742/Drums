# Year 3, Spring

**UNT equivalent:** Level 4.1  
**Focus:** Modern jazz and progressive metal: odd groupings and illusions.

## UNT courses this mirrors
- Applied Drum Set
- MUJS 3370 Advanced Jazz Improvisation II
- MUJS 3620 Jazz Arranging II
- MUMH 3520 Issues in Western Music History
- Jazz ensemble or combo

## Goals
- Odd time in 7/8, 9/8, 11/8
- Polymeter and metric modulation
- Modern jazz drummer vocabulary
- Write an original tune

## Drum set (applied lessons)

### Unit 1: Odd eighth-note meters (weeks 1-3)
- [ ] 7/8, 9/8, and 11/8 grooves with different groupings
- [ ] Solo over a vamp in 7/8
- [ ] Play along with a prog track (Tool 'Schism' or Dream Theater)

### Unit 2: Illusions (weeks 4-6)
- [ ] Gavin Harrison, Rhythmic Illusions: first chapters
- [ ] Polymeter: groupings of 5 or 7 over 4/4 (Meshuggah-style)
- [ ] Metric modulation: swing quarter to dotted-quarter pulse

### Unit 3: Modern jazz (weeks 7-9)
- [ ] Study Brian Blade, Bill Stewart, Jeff 'Tain' Watts
- [ ] Transcribe 16 bars from a modern drummer of your choice
- [ ] Afro-Cuban: Mozambique and cascara with clave

### Unit 4: Feet (weeks 10-12)
- [ ] Bass drum endurance: 16ths at 100-140 bpm (double pedal if you have one)
- [ ] Heel-toe or slide technique basics
- [ ] Hi-hat foot splashes and patterns under grooves

## Musicianship (theory, ear, keyboard)
- [ ] Advanced Improv II: compose a drum solo with a clear shape
- [ ] Arranging II: write an original tune (lead sheet)
- [ ] Music history: how rhythm in Western music shaped jazz and rock

## Listening
- Joshua Redman, Wish (Billy Higgins) and Brian Blade Fellowship
- Branford Marsalis, Crazy People Music (Jeff 'Tain' Watts)
- Porcupine Tree, Fear of a Blank Planet (Gavin Harrison)

## Tunes to learn
- Your original tune
- Footprints in 7/8
- Afro Blue
- Nefertiti

## Jury (weeks 13-16)
Record yourself. Pass every item before moving on.

- [ ] Grooves and fills in 7/8, 9/8, 11/8
- [ ] Demonstrate a polymeter and a metric modulation
- [ ] Perform your original tune (with play-along or band)
- [ ] Play the modern drummer transcription
- [ ] Mozambique groove with clave
