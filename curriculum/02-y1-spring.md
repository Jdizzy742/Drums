# Year 1, Spring

**UNT equivalent:** Level 2.1  
**Focus:** Big band drumming and the shuffle family.

## UNT courses this mirrors
- Applied Drum Set
- MUTH 1600/1700 Theory II + Aural Skills II
- MUJS 1132 Jazz Performance Fundamentals II
- Keyboard Skills II
- Large ensemble (lab band)

## Goals
- Read a big band chart and set up figures
- Shuffle and triplet feels
- Ballad brushes
- Afro-Cuban 6/8 basics

## Drum set (applied lessons)

### Unit 1: Shuffles and triplets (weeks 1-3)
- [ ] Syncopation (Reed) shuffle and triplet exercises (UNT assigns p.10 here), 80-120 bpm
- [ ] Texas shuffle and half-time shuffle grooves
- [ ] Stick Control triplet section, 80 bpm

### Unit 2: Big band reading (weeks 4-7)
- [ ] Short vs long figures: how to play each on the kit
- [ ] Set-ups: one-beat and two-beat setups into ensemble hits
- [ ] Ed Soph, Big Band Primer (or Steve Houghton, Studio and Big Band Drumming): read 3 charts
- [ ] Play along with a Basie recording reading the chart

### Unit 3: Brushes and up-tempo (weeks 8-10)
- [ ] Ballad brushes: circular sweep at 50-70 bpm
- [ ] Up-tempo ride: push from 180 to 220 bpm, relaxed
- [ ] Two-feel vs four-feel: switch on the bridge

### Unit 4: Afro-Cuban 6/8 and rudiments (weeks 11-12)
- [ ] Bembe bell pattern in 6/8 with bass drum and hi-hat
- [ ] Clave: son and rumba, 2-3 and 3-2
- [ ] Wilcoxon All-American Drummer solos 6-12

## Musicianship (theory, ear, keyboard)
- [ ] Theory II: diatonic seventh chords, chord symbols, ii-V-I
- [ ] Aural II: identify maj7, m7, dom7 by ear
- [ ] Keyboard II: ii-V-I shell voicings (root, 3rd, 7th) in C, F, Bb
- [ ] Transcribe: Philly Joe Jones 4-bar trades on a Miles tune

## Listening
- Count Basie, Basie Straight Ahead (Harold Jones)
- Thad Jones/Mel Lewis Orchestra, Consummation (Mel Lewis)
- Duke Ellington, Ellington at Newport (Sam Woodyard)

## Tunes to learn
- Blue Bossa
- St. Thomas
- Footprints
- Take the A Train

## Jury (weeks 13-16)
Record yourself. Pass every item before moving on.

- [ ] Read and play a big band chart with a recording
- [ ] Swing time at 220 bpm, 2 choruses
- [ ] Shuffle groove and ballad brushes
- [ ] 6/8 Afro-Cuban groove with clave
- [ ] Sight-read a short big band excerpt
