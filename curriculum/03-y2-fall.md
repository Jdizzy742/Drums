# Year 2, Fall

**UNT equivalent:** Level 2.2  
**Focus:** Independence and melodic soloing. Add funk and gospel.

## UNT courses this mirrors
- Applied Drum Set
- MUJS 2400/2410 Jazz Theory III + Aural Skills III
- MUJS Jazz Improvisation I
- MUJS 1013 Keyboard Skills III
- Jazz ensemble or combo

## Goals
- Four-way coordination under the ride
- Solo over the form of a tune
- Jazz waltz
- Funk ghost notes and linear grooves

## Drum set (applied lessons)

### Unit 1: Independence (weeks 1-3)
- [ ] Riley, Art of Bop Drumming: snare/bass drum coordination studies
- [ ] Wilcoxon rudimental patterns with feet added (UNT pairs Wilcoxon with foot patterns here)
- [ ] Hi-hat on all 4 beats and on 1 and 3 variations

### Unit 2: Melodic soloing (weeks 4-6)
- [ ] Solo over 12-bar blues keeping the form, singing the melody in your head
- [ ] Motif development: take one 2-beat idea and vary it for a chorus
- [ ] Transcribe and play Max Roach trades on 'Blues Walk' (Clifford Brown and Max Roach)

### Unit 3: Waltz and Latin (weeks 7-9)
- [ ] Jazz waltz ride pattern and comping, 120-180 bpm
- [ ] Songo groove (Soph Big Band Primer or Malabe/Weiner, Afro-Cuban Rhythms for Drumset)
- [ ] Samba with brushes

### Unit 4: Funk and gospel (weeks 10-12)
- [ ] Ghost notes: 16th-note grooves with accented backbeat, 80-100 bpm
- [ ] Purdie shuffle (half-time triplet ghost groove)
- [ ] Linear grooves (David Garibaldi, Future Sounds: first section)
- [ ] Gospel basics: 6-stroke roll as a fill around the kit

## Musicianship (theory, ear, keyboard)
- [ ] Jazz Theory III: chord-scale relationships, modes, tritone substitution
- [ ] Aural III: sing roots of a ii-V-I, hear the bridge of rhythm changes
- [ ] Improv I: learn and sing 3 bebop heads by memory
- [ ] Keyboard III: shell voicings in all 12 keys (slowly)

## Listening
- Clifford Brown and Max Roach, Study in Brown (Max Roach)
- Bill Evans, Sunday at the Village Vanguard (Paul Motian)
- Tower of Power, Tower of Power (David Garibaldi)

## Tunes to learn
- All the Things You Are
- Solar
- Oleo
- Cantaloupe Island

## Jury (weeks 13-16)
Record yourself. Pass every item before moving on.

- [ ] Solo one chorus over blues and one over AABA, form intact
- [ ] Jazz waltz with comping, 2 choruses
- [ ] Songo and samba with brushes
- [ ] One funk groove with ghost notes, one linear groove
- [ ] Play the Max Roach transcription with the record
