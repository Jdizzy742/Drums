# Semester 0: Pre-College Prep

**UNT equivalent:** Deficient Level  
**Focus:** Hands, time, and reading. Get to audition-ready basics.

## UNT courses this mirrors
- Pre-audition preparation (UNT requires students below Level 1 to pass Deficient barriers first)

## Goals
- Play steady time with a click without drifting
- Read quarter, eighth, and simple syncopated rhythms
- Play a basic jazz ride pattern with hi-hat on 2 and 4
- Know the form of a 12-bar blues

## Drum set (applied lessons)

### Unit 1: Grip and strokes (weeks 1-3)
- [ ] Matched grip, fulcrum, and rebound on a practice pad (check in a mirror)
- [ ] Four basic strokes: full, down, tap, up. 5 min daily, quarter = 60-80
- [ ] Single strokes (RLRL) eighth notes, 60 to 100 bpm
- [ ] Double strokes (RRLL) eighth notes, 60 to 90 bpm
- [ ] Kit setup: throne height, pedal and cymbal positions, basic tuning

### Unit 2: Time and basic grooves (weeks 4-6)
- [ ] Eighth-note rock groove, 70 to 110 bpm, 2 min unbroken with click
- [ ] Bass drum variations: 8 patterns from Syncopation (Reed) bass drum studies or similar
- [ ] One-bar fills on snare and toms that land on beat 1 cleanly
- [ ] Click on 2 and 4 only: feel it as the backbeat for 1 minute

### Unit 3: Reading (weeks 7-9)
- [ ] Count out loud: quarters, eighths, rests, ties (1 e & a)
- [ ] Syncopation (Reed): first quarter- and eighth-note reading lessons on snare, 60-80 bpm
- [ ] Stick Control (Stone) p.5 exercises 1-12 at 80 bpm
- [ ] Rudiments: paradiddle, flam, drag, five-stroke roll, slow and even

### Unit 4: First jazz time (weeks 10-12)
- [ ] Jazz ride pattern (spang-spang-a-lang) with hi-hat on 2 and 4, 100-140 bpm
- [ ] Feather the bass drum on all 4 beats (barely audible)
- [ ] Play time through a 12-bar blues play-along, counting bars out loud
- [ ] Trade 4s: 4 bars time, 4 bars simple fill, keep the form

## Musicianship (theory, ear, keyboard)
- [ ] Learn note names on the treble and bass clef (musictheory.net lessons)
- [ ] Learn the major scale construction (W-W-H-W-W-W-H)
- [ ] Learn song form: 12-bar blues and 32-bar AABA

## Listening
- Miles Davis, Kind of Blue (Jimmy Cobb)
- Art Blakey and the Jazz Messengers, Moanin' (Art Blakey)
- Count Basie, The Atomic Mr. Basie (Sonny Payne)

## Tunes to learn
- C Jam Blues
- Sonnymoon for Two
- Moanin'

## Jury (weeks 13-16)
Record yourself. Pass every item before moving on.

- [ ] Rock groove, 100 bpm, 2 min with click, with a fill every 8 bars
- [ ] Jazz time over 3 choruses of blues at 120 bpm with a play-along, no lost form
- [ ] Singles, doubles, paradiddles on pad at 90 bpm eighth notes
- [ ] Read one Syncopation (Reed) page at 70 bpm without stopping
- [ ] Record the jury and keep the video in your log
