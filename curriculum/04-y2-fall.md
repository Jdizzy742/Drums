# Year 2, Fall

**Level:** UNT Level 2.2  
**Focus:** Garibaldi funk, Elvin's triplets, boogaloo, and modal playing.  
**Source:** UNT 2022-23 drum set syllabus

## Courses this mirrors
- MUAC 15xx Lessons
- MULB 18xx Large Ensemble
- MUJS 2400 Jazz Theory III
- MUJS 2410 Jazz Aural Skills III
- MUJS 1013 Jazz Keyboard Skills III
- MUJS 2360 Jazz Improvisation I
- MUET 3060 African-American Music

## Barrier materials
- Garibaldi, Future Sounds p.24, 25, 32, 33, 35-38, 41-44
- Davis, Triplets Between the Limbs (UNT handout), 1-34
- All tunes and play-alongs

## Goals
- Linear funk grooves from Future Sounds
- Triplets split between all four limbs
- Boogaloo and straight-eighth jazz grooves
- Elvin Jones triplet feel on modal tunes

## Weekly lessons

### Week 1
- [ ] Garibaldi p.32-33
- [ ] Triplets Between the Limbs, quarter = 50-60 (metronome always on upbeats)
- [ ] Tune: Cheryl
- [ ] Play-along: The Sidewinder (boogaloo)

- [ ] Garibaldi p.35-36
- [ ] Triplets, 60-70 bpm
- [ ] Tune: Bye-Ya

- [ ] Garibaldi p.37-38
- [ ] Triplets, 70-80 bpm

- [ ] Garibaldi p.41-42

## Musicianship (classroom courses)
- [ ] Jazz Theory III (MUJS 2400): chord-scale relationships, modes, tritone substitution
- [ ] Jazz Aural Skills III (MUJS 2410): hear ii-V-I roots and common forms
- [ ] Jazz Keyboard III (MUJS 1013): shell voicings in all 12 keys
- [ ] Jazz Improvisation I (MUJS 2360): learn and sing 3 bebop heads
- [ ] African-American Music (MUET 3060): blues, gospel, R&B, and funk listening timeline

## Electives (your interests, not UNT)
- [ ] Gospel basics: 6-stroke roll as a fill around the kit
- [ ] Purdie shuffle (half-time triplet ghost groove)

## Listening
- Lee Morgan, The Sidewinder (Billy Higgins)
- John Coltrane, Coltrane Jazz / Coltrane (Elvin Jones)
- Tower of Power, Tower of Power (David Garibaldi)
- Thelonious Monk, Genius of Modern Music (Bye-Ya)
