# Year 2, Spring

**Level:** UNT Level 3.1  
**Focus:** Brazilian styles, region by region.  
**Source:** UNT 2022-23 drum set syllabus

## Courses this mirrors
- MUAC 15xx Lessons
- MULB 18xx Large Ensemble
- MUJS 2500 Jazz Theory IV
- MUJS 2510 Jazz Aural Skills IV
- MUJS 1014 Jazz Keyboard Skills IV
- MUJS 2370 Jazz Improvisation II
- MUJS 3070 History of Jazz
- Upper Divisional Exam (UDE) and Piano Proficiency Exam (PPE)

## Barrier materials
- Alberto Netto, Brazilian Rhythms for Drum Set (assigned pages)
- Guilherme Castro, Brazilian Rhythm Section Training (assigned pages)
- Aponte, Styles (UNT handout)
- Sher Music, The Latin Jazz Real Book

## Goals
- Samba, bossa nova, and partido alto on the kit
- Northeastern styles: baiao, frevo, maracatu
- Bahia styles: samba reggae, afoxe
- Pass the sophomore gates (UDE and piano proficiency)

## Weekly lessons

### Week 1: Brazilian rhythm fundamentals
- [ ] Brazilian rhythmic and feel fundamentals
- [ ] Netto p.55; Castro p.13-16
- [ ] Tune: Sambadouro (LRB p.445)

### Week 2-3: Samba styles
- [ ] Samba drum set orchestration options
- [ ] Netto p.51-55; Castro p.40-42
- [ ] Tune: Aquarela do Brasil (LRB p.49) or So Danco Samba (LRB p.471)

### Week 4: Bossa nova
- [ ] Bossa nova drum set orchestration options
- [ ] Netto p.44-47; Castro p.55-59, 63-65
- [ ] Tune: Wave, Corcovado, or Meu Canario Vizinho Azul (LRB p.333)

### Week 5: Bossa nova brushes
- [ ] Bossa nova brush applications
- [ ] Netto p.48

### Week 6: Samba in odd meters (3, 5, 7)
- [ ] Netto p.50
- [ ] Tune: Cravo e Canela, Five Four (Flora Purim), or Tombo in 7/4 (LRB p.509)

### Week 7: Partido alto
- [ ] Netto p.56-59; Castro p.52-55
- [ ] Tune: Partido Alto (Airto), Jogral (LRB p.249), or Coisa Feita (LRB p.129)

### Week 8: Baiao (Northeast, forro)
- [ ] Netto p.73-76; Castro p.101-103, 106-107
- [ ] Tune: Ponteio (LRB p.411), Kalinda (LRB p.257), or Sorriso de Samanta

### Week 9: Frevo (Northeast)
- [ ] Netto p.88-90
- [ ] Tune: Frevo (LRB p.229) or Frevo Mulher

### Week 10: Maracatu (Afro-Brazilian)
- [ ] Netto p.94-96; Castro p.91-94, 98-99
- [ ] Tune: Maracatudo (Sergio Mendes) or similar

### Week 11: Samba reggae (Bahia)
- [ ] Castro p.76-82
- [ ] Tune: Canto da Cidade or Olodum recordings

### Week 12: Afoxe (Bahia)
- [ ] Netto p.91-93; Castro p.71-75
- [ ] Tune: Filhos de Gandhi, Lua Soberana (LRB p.309), or Sina (Djavan)

### Week 13
- [ ] Review for jury
