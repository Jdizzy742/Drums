# Year 4, Spring

**Level:** Senior Recital  
**Focus:** The senior recital.  
**Source:** UNT 2022-23 drum set syllabus

## Courses this mirrors
- MUJS 4720 Jazz Senior Recital Capstone
- MUJS 3800 Conducting Jazz Ensembles
- MUCM 3550 Jazz Chamber Music
- MULB 18xx Large Ensemble
- No applied lessons this term

## Barrier materials
- Senior recital performed

## Goals
- Perform a 45-minute recital
- Show range: swing, brushes, Brazilian, Afro-Cuban, funk, odd meter, an original
- Record it as your capstone

## Weekly lessons

### Week 1-6: Rehearse
- [ ] Weekly run-throughs of the full program
- [ ] Book a venue, band, and date

### Week 7-10: Preview
- [ ] Preview performance for friends or a teacher
- [ ] Fix issues from the preview

### Week 11-14: Perform
- [ ] Perform the recital
- [ ] Record and archive it

## Musicianship (classroom courses)
- [ ] Conducting Jazz Ensembles (MUJS 3800): beat patterns in 2, 3, 4; cue a big band chart
- [ ] Write program notes for each tune

## Listening
- Re-listen to every album from Prep 1. Notice what you hear now.

- Your recital program

## Jury
Record yourself. Pass every item before moving on.
