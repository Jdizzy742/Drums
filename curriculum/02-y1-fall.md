# Year 1, Fall

**Level:** UNT Level 1  
**Focus:** Big band reading, Alan Dawson vocabulary, and the first Latin flows.  
**Source:** UNT 2022-23 drum set syllabus

## Courses this mirrors
- MUAC 15xx Lessons
- MULB 18xx Large Ensemble
- MUTH 1400 Theory I
- MUTH 1410 Aural Skills I
- MUAG 1011 Keyboard Skills I
- MUJS 1131 Jazz Performance Fundamentals

## Barrier materials
- Helbing, Big Band Sight Reading (etudes 1, 12, 16, 17, 20, 21)
- Davis, Single Stroke Pyramid (UNT handout), feet patterns 1-9
- Reed, Syncopation p.38-41
- Davis, Vocabulary #1 (UNT handout), 1-8
- All play-alongs and tunes

## Goals
- Reed p.38 with ride pattern up to 150 bpm
- Read and play big band etudes
- Memorize Vocabulary #1 and solo with it
- Samba and songo flows on tunes

## Weekly lessons

### Week 1
- [ ] Single Stroke Pyramid with feet patterns 1 and 2, 80 bpm
- [ ] Helbing Big Band etude 1
- [ ] Ramsay (Alan Dawson vocabulary) p.11-12
- [ ] Reed p.38 at 120 bpm with quarter notes and ride pattern on ride (left hand alone, snare/bass alternating and unison)
- [ ] Play-along: Wives and Lovers (swing)
- [ ] Tune: Anthropology (full tune routine)

### Week 2
- [ ] Pyramid, feet patterns 1 and 2, 90 bpm
- [ ] Ramsay p.13-14

## Musicianship (classroom courses)
- [ ] Theory I (MUTH 1400): intervals, scales, triads, seventh chords
- [ ] Aural Skills I (MUTH 1410): sing scales and intervals, rhythmic dictation
- [ ] Keyboard Skills I (MUAG 1011): major scales, basic triads
- [ ] Jazz Performance Fundamentals (MUJS 1131): sing and play tune melodies by ear
- [ ] Large ensemble: play with a community big band, or full Basie charts with recordings
